Popular Locations for Office Space in Ahmedabad

When considering office space for rent in Ahmedabad, understanding various locations can significantly impact your decision. Here’s a look at some prime areas:

1. SG Highway
SG Highway is the epicenter of business activity, housing numerous multinational corporations and startups. The availability of modern facilities makes it a favorite among tech companies.

2. Prahlad Nagar
Prahlad Nagar boasts a mix of commercial and residential properties, ideal for businesses looking to attract talent with an appealing work-life balance.

3. Ashram Road
Known for its strategic position, Ashram Road is a historical area that attracts businesses due to its proximity to banks, hotels, and public transportation.

4. Naroda
As an industrial hub, Naroda offers affordable office spaces, making it suitable for manufacturing and logistics companies.

5. Bopal
This suburban area is rapidly developing and is perfect for businesses seeking a quiet environment without sacrificing connectivity.

Cost Considerations for Office Space in Ahmedabad

The cost of office space for rent in Ahmedabad can vary widely based on location, size, and amenities. Here’s a breakdown of what to expect:


  Budget Options: Starting from ₹20,000/month for smaller spaces in less central areas.
  Mid-Range: Ranges from ₹40,000 to ₹80,000/month for well-located offices with basic amenities.
  Premium Spaces: High-end office spaces can cost upwards of ₹1,00,000/month in prime areas like SG Highway and Ashram Road.


It's essential to assess the total cost of occupancy, including maintenance fees and utility costs, when budgeting for an office space.
